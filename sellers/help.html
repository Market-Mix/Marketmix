Seller Help Center

Browse seller-focused guides, FAQs, and support options to manage your shop with confidence.

Account, Login & Store Setup

Need to update your profile, reset your password, or configure store details? Go to Shop Settings to manage your seller profile, payout information, and shipping address.

  • Change store name, logo, or business details.
  • Use password reset on the login page if you cannot sign in.
  • Set up payout details before receiving order payments.
Products & Inventory

Add new products and keep inventory accurate from My Products. Make sure product titles, descriptions, and prices are complete so shoppers can find your listings.

  • Update stock levels whenever inventory changes.
  • Use rich images and clear descriptions to boost sales.
  • Mark products out of stock to avoid overselling.
Orders, Fulfillment & Shipping

Manage incoming orders from Orders. Confirm shipments promptly and update tracking information to keep buyers informed.

  • Ship orders within your stated handling time.
  • Mark completed or cancelled orders correctly.
  • Contact buyers through order messages when needed.
Returns, Refunds & Disputes

Handle return requests and refund workflows in Returns & Refunds. Follow the platform policy to keep disputes fair and resolve buyer issues quickly.

  • Review each return request before approving.
  • Issue refunds only after receiving returned items as required.
  • Respond to disputes through the seller dashboard.
Payments & Payouts

Confirm your payout information is correct under Shop Settings. Payments are issued according to our payout schedule after orders are completed.

  • Update bank or mobile money details before store activation.
  • Track payment status via your earnings and order reports.
  • Contact support if you notice any missing payout.

How do I update my store payout details?

Open Shop Settings and update your payout details. Save changes and verify your bank or mobile wallet to receive payouts.

What should I do if an order is delayed?

Check the order status in Orders and update tracking information if available. Contact the buyer and support if the delay persists.

How can I manage product stock levels?

Go to My Products and edit the product listing to update inventory. Mark items out of stock when they are unavailable.

Can I request help for returns and refunds?

Yes. Visit Returns & Refunds and review pending returns. If you need assistance, contact support using the buttons above.

Still need help?
