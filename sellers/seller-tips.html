Seller Tips

Practical tips to help you increase sales and run a successful shop on MarketMix.

1. Use high-quality photos

Clear, well-lit photos increase buyer trust and conversion rates.

2. Write helpful product descriptions

Include measurements, materials, and care instructions where relevant.

3. Competitive pricing and promotions

Monitor similar items and offer discounts or bundles to stand out.

4. Fast shipping and clear policies

Provide accurate shipping times and transparent return policies.
