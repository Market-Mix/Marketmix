Help Center

Your buyer support hub for orders, payments, returns, account help, and quick support access.

🛍️ Orders & Tracking

How can I track my order? — Go to “My Orders” and click “Track Order” to view the delivery status.

Can I cancel an order? — Orders can only be canceled before they are shipped.

What if my order didn’t arrive? — Contact our support team through the hub below.

💳 Payments & Billing

Which payment methods are accepted? — We accept credit/debit cards, PayPal, and MarketMix Wallet.

Why was my payment declined? — Check your card details or balance and try again.

How do I update billing information? — Visit Account Settings and select “Payment Methods.”

📦 Returns & Refunds

How do I return a product? — Go to “My Orders” → select the item → click “Request Return.”

How long do refunds take? — Refunds are processed within 5–10 business days.

Can I track my refund? — Yes, your refund status appears under “Returns & Refunds.”

👤 Account & Settings

How do I reset my password? — Click “Forgot Password” on the login page to reset it.

Can I change my email address? — Yes, under Account Settings → “Edit Email.”

How do I update my delivery address? — Edit your profile address details in Account Settings.

🛒 Selling on MarketMix

How do I open a shop? — Go to the seller signup page and follow the steps to create your store.

How do I list a new product? — From your seller dashboard, click “Add Product.”

How do I manage store orders? — Use your seller dashboard to view and process incoming orders.

📞 Contact Support

Need personalized help? Email marketmixhq@gmail.com for buyer support or submit a support request below.

Average response time: Within 24 hours on business days.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions
How do I create an account on MarketMix?
Creating an account is easy! Click “Sign Up” on the homepage, fill in your details, and verify your email.
Can I sell products on MarketMix?
Yes! Go to “Own a Shop” or “Become a Seller,” fill in your shop details, and upload products after approval.
How do I track my orders?
After your order is confirmed, visit your dashboard and select “Track Orders” for delivery status.
What payment methods are accepted?
We accept debit/credit cards, bank transfers, and secure wallet payments via encrypted gateways.
How do I request a return or refund?
Go to “Returns & Refunds” in your account, submit a request within the return window, and our team will assist.
Is my personal information safe?
Yes, MarketMix uses advanced encryption to protect your data and never shares it without consent.

Still need help?

Our customer support team is ready to assist with any order, payment, or account issue. Reach out and we’ll respond as soon as possible.
