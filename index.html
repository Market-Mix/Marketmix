MarketMix supports multiple payment methods including bank transfers, card payments, and mobile money. All transactions are SSL-encrypted and protected by our buyer guarantee.

Sellers can request withdrawals from their available balance at any time via the Earnings dashboard. Funds are typically processed within 1–3 business days depending on your bank.

To become a verified seller, you'll need to complete our KYC process by submitting a valid government-issued ID and a selfie. Verification typically takes 24–48 hours and gives your store a trusted badge.

If your order doesn't arrive or isn't as described, you can submit a return/refund report within 24 hours of confirming delivery. Our support team reviews each case and resolves it fairly for both parties.

Creating a store on MarketMix is completely free. A small commission is applied on successful sales to maintain the platform and support services. Full fee details are available in your seller settings.
